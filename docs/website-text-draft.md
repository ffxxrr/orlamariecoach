### My Story
My interest in meditation began at sixteen, when I discovered a book on meditation that belonged to my father. What started as curiosity became a daily practice that has stayed with me for thirty years.

Living in Donegal, close to the Atlantic coast and open landscape, I learned early how much a few moments of stillness can change the way we meet the day. That experience led me to train professionally, so I could share mindfulness safely and effectively with others.

I am an accredited mindfulness teacher, trained with Mindfulness Now, The UK College of Mindfulness Meditation. My teaching draws on the established, evidence-based mindfulness approaches developed over the past forty years. It is secular, practical and adapted to the people in front of me.

Today I deliver mindfulness programmes for workplaces, schools and individuals, in person and online. My aim is simple: to give people practical skills they can use for life.

### Frequently Asked Questions
**What is mindfulness?**
Mindfulness means paying attention to the present moment, on purpose and without judgement. Through simple meditation practices, such as focusing on the breath, the body and everyday activities, we learn to notice thoughts and feelings without being carried away by them. It is a secular, practical skill that anyone can learn.

**Is this suitable for beginners?**
Yes. All programmes are designed for people with no previous experience of meditation. Each practice is introduced step by step, and you'll be supported throughout.

**What does the research say?**
Mindfulness-based approaches have been studied extensively over the past forty years. Evidence suggests they can help reduce stress and support wellbeing, focus and resilience.

**Can mindfulness replace therapy or medical treatment?**
No. Mindfulness can be a valuable support, but it is not a substitute for medical or psychological care. If you are going through significant mental health difficulties, we'll talk beforehand to make sure a programme is suitable for you. I'd also encourage you to speak with your GP.

**Are online sessions as effective as in-person?**
Online sessions work very well for mindfulness. Many people find it helpful to practise in their own environment. Online delivery also makes it easy for teams in different locations to take part.

**How do I arrange a programme for my workplace or school?**
Get in touch with a short outline of your organisation and what you'd like to achieve. I'll arrange a call to discuss your needs and then send a tailored proposal.
